## 5. Distribution of gains and losses

Households may gain contestable records; operators may gain clearer reviews, but false alarms add work. Security employment may shift toward verification and maintenance; automation must not be presented as a proved labour saving. Armed response remains a separately accountable human service. Insurers might improve dispute processing, but risk pricing could penalise households declining monitoring: prohibit coercive data access in proposed contracts. Crime displacement cannot be determined from retrospective aggregate claims or a forecast that loses to baseline. A prospective pilot needs comparison areas and harm monitoring before impact claims.

## 6. Payments, tax and consumer operations

| Method | Practical tradeoff | Proposed handling |
|---|---|---|
| Debit order | Consent and failed collections; failure rate unknown | Obtain mandate via provider; measure failures, do not invent market rate |
| Card | Convenient recurring payment; expiry/fees | Hosted provider checkout, no card data stored by VUKA |
| EFT | Accessible but reconciliation delays | Unique invoice reference; reconcile before marking paid |
| Prepaid voucher | Can suit irregular income; distribution/fraud cost | Pilot only after refund, resale and expiry terms reviewed |

Proposed missed-payment policy (ASSUMPTION): contact privately, offer 30-day grace funded in reserve, keep local installed functions and access to existing records, clearly disclose any cessation of staffed response with notice and handover. Do not silently disable protection or hold subject rights hostage to payment. This policy is not implemented or financed.

SARS currently states 15% VAT and compulsory registration above R2.3m taxable sales in an uninterrupted prior 12-month period ([SARS guidance](https://www.sars.gov.za/faq/what-is-vat-and-who-needs-to-register/)). If R299 is VAT-inclusive after registration: net sale = R299/1.15 = R260; VAT = R39. With costs conservatively unchanged at R162, gross contribution = R98. Input-tax recovery requires invoices and accountant review; the supplied model's tax basis is unresolved. Do not advertise R137 as a post-VAT margin.

PSiRA identifies electronic security/monitoring services in its [consumer notice](https://www.psira.co.za/dmdocuments/circular/Industry_Circular-Consumer_Information_Notice-Government_Sector_and_General.pdf). Obtain a scope determination for installation, monitoring and response; “software only” does not settle it. Counsel must review cancellation, plain-language terms, product safety, unfair terms, marketing and remedies against the [Consumer Protection Act](https://www.gov.za/documents/consumer-protection-act). No commercial launch before that review.
