# P2.4 — POPIA s57(1)(a) decision record

Status: **open; legal/privacy determination required before pilot.**

The proposed data flow may link unique identifiers contributed by separate responsible parties (for example, household sightings and subject records) for a new purpose. POPIA s57(1)(a) prior authorisation is therefore a live gate. This record does not assert that the section applies or that discard-by-default embeddings remove the authorisation question.

Before pilot approval, Ipeleng and counsel must record:

1. whether the planned linkage is a new purpose and whether the parties are separate responsible parties;
2. which data elements are unique identifiers and whether any criminal-behaviour information is processed on behalf of a third party;
3. whether an exemption applies, with its exact legal basis; and
4. if authorisation is required, the application owner, submission date, conditions, and approval evidence.

Until those fields are answered, the safe operating boundary is tenant isolation, no cross-household identity linking, no public-chain personal data, and no pilot launch. The discard-by-default embedding boundary is documented separately in `docs/DISCARD-BY-DEFAULT-EMBEDDINGS.md`.
