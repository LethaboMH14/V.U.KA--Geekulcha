How it works

  1. Step 1

    Recorded on the phone

    VIGIL signs each check-in with a key held on the member's device, and every entry names the hash of the one before it.

  2. Step 2

    Fingerprinted on Hedera

    The ANCHOR server writes one 33-byte Merkle root per window of up to a minute to a public Hedera topic, which fixes its time and order.

  3. Step 3

    Checked by anyone

    A bank or insurer drops the member's shared record into Verify, and their own browser recomputes every hash and matches the root on Hedera.

For banks and insurers

A member's record reaches you as a file. You check it yourself, in your browser, against a public ledger we cannot edit.

What you receive
A record the member chooses to share from VIGIL, under Settings → My record → Share with a bank or insurer. It is one JSON file.
What you do
Open Verify and drop the file. Your browser recomputes every hash and signature and checks the root on Hedera. Nothing is uploaded.
What you file
The fingerprint: the Merkle root, the chain head, the topic, the sequence number and the consensus time. Anyone can check it against the public topic later.

A match proves

  • These exact entries existed, in this order, no later than the topic's consensus time.
  • Each entry was signed by the key registered for it, and the server key is the pinned one.
  • Nobody has edited, removed or reordered an entry since.

It does not prove

  • That the event was real. A signature shows a key was used, not what a microphone heard.
  • That nothing is missing from before the export, or that a compromised server sent every alert.
  • Permanence on testnet. Hedera resets testnet; a re-anchor after a reset is a later record under a new epoch.

Privacy and duress

A duress PIN is recorded inside a signed commitment, so the chain shows only a hash. The public topic shows at most one root per minute, so anyone watching sees that something happened in that minute, never whether it was a duress check-in or a normal one. Nothing personal goes on-chain: each Hedera message is a type byte and a 32-byte fingerprint.
