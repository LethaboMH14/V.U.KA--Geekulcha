## 1 · What VUKA can honestly offer, and what it can't

Every conversation starts here. Over-promising to police or to survivors' organisations does lasting damage.

| VUKA does | VUKA does not |
|---|---|
| Notices possible duress on the member's phone and asks a discreet check-in | Dispatch police, security or anyone else (no autonomous dispatch, `RULES.md`) |
| Alerts the guardians the member chose, today with **"Don't call or text them. Call 10111."** | Make police arrive faster. Its honest value is that **guardians learn sooner** |
| Keeps a signed record whose timing a stranger can check (**when, not what**) | Prove that duress happened, or identify an attacker |
| Lets the member export their own record | Push anything to SAPS, or hand member data to police without the member's own export or a logged lawful order |

**Why "Call 10111" needs company** (`REPORTED`, parliamentary reply CW502, 8 Jul 2026, https://static.pmg.org.za/RCW502-2026-07-08.pdf):
- 10111's national average response time to the most urgent ("Alpha") complaints was **28 minutes 30 seconds** as at 30 Apr 2026.
- **2,008,711 emergency calls went unanswered** in 2025/26.
- Sometimes the danger is a police officer. About 95–97 SAPS members were accused of rape or sexual violence in 2024/25 (EWN, 22 Feb 2026), and a Hawks officer was arrested for kidnapping and extortion on 22 Sep 2026.

**Proposed change** (Lethabo decides; it is app copy): keep "Call 10111", and add non-police routes on the guardian's screen: the GBV Command Centre (0800 428 428), the TEARS Foundation USSD line (`*134*7355#`), and Campus Protection when the member is on campus. Never imply faster police response.

## 2 · Order of approach

The research confirms the order NGOs → campus → SAPS, and sharpens it.

1. **CPSI now, for a debrief only.** The Centre for Public Service Innovation co-hosted the Geekulcha hackathon (`FACT`, `docs/MASTER-CONTEXT.md` §1), and replicating innovations across the public sector is part of its mandate. It is the only warm government door.
   - Ask which SAPS or Civilian Secretariat innovation contact to approach, when, and with what evidence.
   - Its innovation awards have required a year of running in the public sector (2014 rules, `REPORTED`), so VUKA isn't eligible yet.
2. **Wits, as the first pilot community, alongside what it already runs.**
   - Wits already has the **mySOS** panic button, routed to Campus Protection Services (`REPORTED`). UCT runs **Kwanele**, and Gauteng runs an **e-Panic Button**.
   - VUKA must complement these, covering coercion where a button can't be pressed, never replace them.
   - Wits students protested over GBV handling in Nov 2025 (`REPORTED`). So a pilot goes through the Gender Equity Office and student bodies, is survivor-led, and is **never marketed on the back of those protests**.
3. **GBV organisations, for design reviews, between 1 and 24 Nov 2026.** That is before the **16 Days of Activism (25 Nov–10 Dec)**, when their staff are busiest. Ask for review, not endorsement; offer something in return for staff time.
4. **SAPS later, at police-station level first, and after mid-November.**
   - **Precedents** (`REPORTED`): Namola signed with Brooklyn SAPS (2017), and Claremont police backed UCT's Kwanele. Industry bodies already hold MoUs with SAPS: BACSA since 2018 and SABRIC since 2001.
   - **Timing** (`REPORTED`): the National Commissioner and the Police Minister are both acting. Local government elections are on **4 Nov 2026**. The Madlanga Commission's final report is due **16 Nov 2026**.
   - A national approach before Q1 2027 is likely to stall (`ASSUMPTION`).
5. **Industry bodies alongside the bank track** ([`01-B2B-ENTERPRISE.md`](01-B2B-ENTERPRISE.md)): SABRIC and BACSA.

## 4 · Risks, and the rules that answer them

- **Sharing data with police.**
  - SAPS itself was found in breach of POPIA over leaked details of the Krugersdorp rape victims, with an enforcement notice in Apr 2023 (`REPORTED`).
  - Information about alleged criminal behaviour is special personal information (POPIA s26), and processing it for third parties may need the Regulator's **prior authorisation** (s57).
  - **Rule:** data reaches police only by the member's own export or a logged lawful order; VUKA pushes nothing to SAPS. Whether an exported record counts as "criminal behaviour" information is a question for a privacy lawyer, a human gate.
- **When the abuser is a guardian, or is police.** Guardian removal is silent and delayed, and duress additions create a decoy (ADR-0036). GBV organisations review this scenario first.
- **Mandatory reporting.** Since 14 Apr 2023, Domestic Violence Act s2B requires any adult who knows of or suspects domestic violence against a child, a person with a disability or an older person to report it (`REPORTED`). Guardian onboarding may need to say so. Activists warn it can override women's autonomy, so this needs legal review before any wording ships.
- **Endorsement conditions.**
  - No "endorsed by", "backed by" or "in partnership with" until the organisation says so in writing.
  - No testing with survivors without the organisation's own safeguarding protocol and informed consent.
  - `sim_` labels on anything that isn't a real alert path.
- **Overlap with incumbents.** mySOS, Kwanele, Gauteng's e-Panic Button, Namola and MySAPS all exist. Each conversation states plainly what VUKA does that they don't: it notices possible duress, its guardians are told, and it **proves when, not what**. Never copy claims about legal admissibility made elsewhere.
